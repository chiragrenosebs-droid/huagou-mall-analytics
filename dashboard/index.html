🛑

Discount Governance Cliff

Margin Protection

Discounts above 30% trigger rapid margin degradation. 4,037 completed order lines (8.18%) generate negative gross margin, wiping out over ¥280K in potential earnings.

Loss-Making Lines 4,037 (8.18%)
Max Discount Observed 64.8%
Recommendation: Implement automated platform guardrails capping promotional discounts at 30% unless explicitly subsidized by merchants.
⚡

The 618 & Double 11 Duopoly

Commercial Seasonality

June (¥2.53M) and November (¥2.51M) generate over 27.6% of annual turnover. Daily revenue during these two peak months spikes from ¥21K/day to over ¥42K/day (1.92× baseline).

June + Nov Share 27.66%
Daily Revenue Index 1.92× Baseline
Recommendation: Concentrate warehouse logistics, inventory pre-orders, and ad spend exclusively around the 4 weeks leading up to June 18 and November 11.
💻

Digital Electronics Dominance

Category Concentration

Digital Electronics produces ¥7.85M (43.09% of all revenue) from 31.38% of lines, driven by high basket items in Computer Accessories (¥2.74M) and Audio Equipment (¥2.33M).

Electronics Revenue ¥7,852,160
Revenue / Line ¥507.34
Recommendation: Cross-sell high-margin accessories (Home & Living, Apparel) alongside high-ticket Digital Electronics baskets to elevate basket margins.
📊

Price & Quantity Independence

Pricing Architecture

Customers purchase similar quantities (1–5 units) regardless of unit price (Spearman ρ = +0.004). Volume does not decline as price ascends.

Price vs Qty Correlation ρ = +0.004
Price Decile Profit Range ¥5.31 → ¥308.37
Recommendation: Do not rely on low-price loss leaders to stimulate quantity; focus on premium bundles where unit economics remain strongly positive.
